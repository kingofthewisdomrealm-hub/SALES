# SALES — The Full Conversation

A **Jeremy Miner–style NEPQ trainer**: you run one sale from the first knock to the signed next step, and you can see **what each person is thinking** while they talk.

Play it: open `index.html` in a browser.

This is **not** a remake of [Knockandrestore](https://github.com/kingofthewisdomrealm-hub/Knockandrestore). That game taught porch pressure with a Game Boy frame, six generic buttons, and a walking street. This trainer teaches the **whole conversation** — connection before situation, problem ownership before the pitch, commitment before the presentation — with thought bubbles, a visible process rail, and a debrief.

## What you practice

1. Approach  
2. Connection  
3. Situation  
4. Problem awareness  
5. Solution awareness  
6. Consequence  
7. Commitment  
8. Presentation  
9. Next step

NEPQ (Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questioning) is used here as a **public teaching model**: questions first, pitch last, buyer says the close. Scripts are original. They are not 7th Level course material.

## How a round works

- Pick a homeowner and a mode (**Learn** shows both thought bubbles + coach notes; **Practice** hides the seller’s inner coach).
- Scene 1 is the porch. Scene 2 is the kitchen table after findings.
- Each turn you choose a line. The engine scores whether it belongs in this phase.
- Thought bubbles show the **unspoken** side: resistance, hope, and what you should be listening for.
- After the last scene you get a debrief: what they owned, where you jumped the process, and why the ending happened.
