# SALES — The Full Conversation

A **Jeremy Miner–style NEPQ trainer**: one sale from the first knock to the next step, with **thought bubbles** over both people.

Play it: open `index.html` in a browser (needs `styles.css`, `data.js`, and `engine.js` next to it).

This is **not** a remake of [Knockandrestore](https://github.com/kingofthewisdomrealm-hub/Knockandrestore). That game taught porch pressure with a Game Boy frame. This trainer teaches the **whole conversation** — questions first, pitch last, they say the close.

## Field talk (Josias, storm 3/16)

Learn this in order. Do not dump Version 4 on a person who only said hello.

1. **Door** — “Hey, I’m Josias with the storm restoration team. We’re doing free inspections for the storm on 3/16. What I’ll do is a quick check for damage, take pictures, and show you what I saw when I’m done. Mind if I take a look?”
2. **Look** — Walk where they point. Pictures only.
3. **Show-back** — “Here’s what I saw.” They name whether it matters.
4. **Claim path** — File a claim. Guide until approved. Work for what they pay, no more no less. Out of pocket is the deductible.
5. **Loop** — Call together if they want. Info in the CRM the same hour. Follow up on the claim.

Do not promise what insurance will pay. Do not invent damage. Do not take a deposit to lock a crew.

## How a trainer round works

- Pick Dale (guarded) or Gene (burned).
- **Learn** shows both thought bubbles and the coach note. **Practice** hides the seller’s inner coach.
- Scene 1 is the porch. A real commitment question unlocks Scene 2 — the kitchen table after findings.
- Each line is scored for phase fit. Thought clouds are the lesson.
- The debrief lists which flags the buyer actually spoke (problem, want, cost, intent).
