# Gradual versions — Josias · storm 3/16 · claim path

Do not learn the whole conversation on day one. Learn one layer. When that layer is clean, add the next.

This sits next to the NEPQ Do / Don't book. That book is how you think. This book is what you actually say after the 3/16 storm.

Word copy with the same content: `Gradual_Versions_Storm_Claim_Path.docx` (project folder).

## How gradual works

- Version 1 is the only thing a new person says at the door.
- Version 2 starts after they say yes to the look.
- Version 3 starts when you come back with pictures.
- Version 4 is the claim path — only after they have seen the photos.
- Version 5 is the paperwork loop — only after they want the claim.

If you skip a version, you sound like a pitch. If their face goes tight, drop back one version and get quiet.

## Simple picture of the whole path

Knock. Ask to look. Take pictures. Show pictures. Help file the claim. Fix what insurance pays. They pay the deductible. Write it down. Check back.

You are Josias. You are not selling a binder. You are walking one storm — March 16 — from the door to a followed-up claim.

---

## Version 1 · The door

**Goal.** Get permission for a free look. Nothing else.

**What you do.** Say who you are. Name the storm date. Promise a short check, pictures, and a show-back. Stop talking.

**Done when.** They let you walk the house, or they say not today and you leave clean.

**Simple.** Hi. My name is Josias. A storm hit on March 16. I can look for free, take pictures, and show you. Is that okay?

**Say**

- Hey — I'm Josias with the storm restoration team.
- We're doing free inspections for the storm on 3/16.
- What I'll do is a quick check for damage, take pictures, and show you what I saw when I'm done.
- I don't need the whole afternoon. Mind if I take a look?

**Never say**

- We're the full-service company — let me tell you about our packages.
- You should file a claim today.
- I can get a crew out this week if you sign.

---

## Version 2 · The look

**Goal.** Collect facts. Do not name the job yet.

**What you do.** Walk where they point. Take pictures. Write what you see in plain words. Do not diagnose from the yard.

**Done when.** You have photos of real marks — missing shingles, lifted flashing, a stain in the hall — and they watched you take them.

**Simple.** Look where they point. Take pictures of the real spots. Do not guess. Do not scare them.

**Say**

- I'll start on the side that took the weather on 3/16.
- If you have a spot inside you've been watching, show me that too.
- I'm only taking pictures of what is here. I'll show you every one.

**Never say**

- That's a full tear-off. I can tell from the grass.
- That's moisture intrusion. You'll have rot.
- Insurance is going to cover the whole roof.

---

## Version 3 · Show what you saw

**Goal.** Put the pictures in their hands. Let them see the problem.

**What you do.** Come back to the porch or the table. One photo at a time. Ask what they notice. Let them name it.

**Done when.** They have said, in their words, that something is wrong — not just that you talked a lot.

**Simple.** Show the pictures. Ask if the spots bother them. They have to say it is a problem.

**Say**

- Here's what I saw.
- This is the south slope. This is the hall mark you already wipe.
- When you look at that — is that just ugly, or is that something you want handled?

**Never say**

- See? You're going to lose the whole roof if you wait.
- Let me run you through every package.
- ACT NOW. Storm season isn't over.

---

## Version 4 · The next step is a claim

**Goal.** Name the path. Do not close the job yet.

**What you do.** Tell them the next honest step: file a claim. You can walk them through it until it is approved. Then the work matches what insurance pays.

**Done when.** They understand: claim first, work after approval, no mystery invoice.

**Simple.** Next we tell the insurance company. We help until they say yes. Then we fix what they agree to pay for. You only pay your deductible.

**Say**

- The next step is to file a claim.
- We can guide you through the process until it gets approved.
- Then we just do the work for what they pay — no more, no less.
- All you pay out of pocket is your deductible.

**Never say**

- Don't worry about insurance. Just sign and we'll figure it out later.
- If you use us we can get you a bigger check.
- I need a deposit today to lock the crew.

Do not promise what insurance will pay. Do not invent damage.

---

## Version 5 · Help them call, write it down, follow up

**Goal.** Leave with a real next action in the CRM. Not a vibe.

**What you do.** If they want help, sit with them for the call. Get the info. Put it in the CRM the same hour. Set the follow-up before you drive off.

**Done when.** Name, phone, address, carrier if they have it, deductible if they know it, photos attached, claim number if they already filed, and a dated follow-up.

**Simple.** Help them make the call. Write their name and number down for real. Check on them later. Do not vanish.

**Say**

- If you want, we can call together right now. I can stay on the line while you file.
- I'll take your name, number, and the address so I can send you the pictures and check on the claim.
- After we hang up I'll put this in our system and follow up with you on the claim — I won't leave you guessing.

**Never say**

- Just call them whenever. I'll swing back sometime.
- Give me a card on file so we can start.
- If you don't file today the money disappears.

## When you stack them

A clean call from door to CRM sounds like this. Pause between versions. Do not recite it as one speech.

| Layer | What it sounds like |
| --- | --- |
| Door | Hey — I'm Josias with the storm restoration team. We're doing free inspections for the storm on 3/16. What I'll do is a quick check for damage, take pictures, and show you what I saw when I'm done. Mind if I take a look? |

## How this maps to the nine steps

| NEPQ step | Version | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 Approach | 1 Door | Name, team, storm date, permission. |
| 1 Connection | 1 Door | Short, calm, no grin speech. |
| 2 Situation | 2 Look | Pictures of what is true. |
| 3 Problem | 3 Show-back | They look at the photo and own it. |
| 4 Solution | 4 Path | Claim → approval → we do that work. |
| 5 Consequence | 3–4 | If they wait, the stain is still the stain. They say that. You don't. |
| 6 Commitment | 4–5 | They agree to file, or they don't. |
| 7 Presentation | 4 Path | Deductible. No more, no less. No binder circus. |
| 8 Next step | 5 Loop | Call together. CRM. Follow up on the claim. |

## CRM card — before you leave the street

If it is not in the CRM the same hour, it did not happen.

- Name and phone
- Address and how to find the house
- Storm date: 3/16
- What you photographed, in their words
- Did they want to file? Yes / not yet / no
- Carrier and claim number if they have one
- Deductible if they know it. Blank is fine
- Photos attached
- Follow-up date and who owns it — you

Do not write “hot lead.” Write the next human action: “Call Thursday 10am to check claim status.”

## Practice order

1. Day one: Version 1 only. Ten doors. Permission or a clean no.
2. Day two: Versions 1–2. Look and pictures. No claim talk.
3. Day three: Add Version 3. Show-back. Stop after they name the problem.
4. Day four: Add Version 4 if they owned the photos.
5. Day five: Add Version 5. Call, CRM, follow-up date.

That is gradual. You earn the next sentence by finishing the last one.
